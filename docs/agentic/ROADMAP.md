# Mini Kargo — Development Roadmap

## Roadmap Overview
The roadmap follows the phased autonomous development campaign defined for Mini Kargo, delivering an evolving, mobile-first, vibrant 3D city experience.

| Phase | Title | Status | Summary |
|-------|-------|--------|---------|
| **PHASE 0** | Audit & Baseline Exploration | ✅ Complete | Complete codebase, district, asset, physics, economy, and browser QA discovery. |
| **PHASE 1** | Critical Stabilization | ✅ Complete | Zero test failures, zero TypeScript errors, clean bundle compilation, backwards-compatible storage. |
| **PHASE 2** | City Architecture & Visual Overhaul | ✅ Complete | 4 distinctive districts (Güneş Mahallesi, Çarşı Bölgesi, Sanayi Bölgesi, Sahil Şeridi) with authentic Kenney 3D assets, seamless road connectivity, and clear delivery points. |
| **PHASE 3** | Living City Simulation | ✅ Complete | 37 traffic vehicles with distance keeping and recoil, 39 pedestrians with animations and dodging, 28 dynamic/breakable props with Web Audio synthesis, 11 atmospheric clouds. |
| **PHASE 4** | Gameplay & Progression Polish | ✅ Complete | Refined vehicle dynamics (van & scooter), fair capsule collision sliding, multi-slot cargo feedback, 5 upgrade tiers, 40-node BFS autonomous courier fleet, offline earnings. |
| **PHASE 5** | Mobile UX & Performance | ✅ Complete | Responsive touch controls (joystick, drift, nitro buttons), safe-area insets, radar minimap, <50 draw calls, 60 FPS mobile target. |
| **PHASE 6** | Quality Refinement & Verification | ✅ Complete | 611/611 automated tests passing, Playwright browser test passing across desktop/mobile viewports with 0 errors, independent Victory Audit confirmed. |
| **PHASE 7** | Continuous Visual Polish & District Detailing | ✅ Complete | Unblocked stunt ramp, corporate monument pylon, BuyPad distance fade & depth occlusion, desktop/mobile control isolation, zero-overlap HUD. |
| **PHASE 8** | Multi-District Landmarks & Stunt Airtime Polish | ✅ Complete | Çarşı market stalls & historic clock tower, Sanayi container gantry crane, Sahil promenade lamps & lifeguard tower, suburban mailboxes, procedural ramp launch/landing audio & stunt airtime rewards. |
| **PHASE 9** | Dynamic Atmosphere, Ambience & Living World Landmarks | ✅ Complete | Dynamic shift time of day cycle (Morning, Midday, Golden Hour Sunset, Twilight Dusk) with sun/sky/fog transitions, procedural Web Audio district soundscapes, maritime lighthouse with rotating beam, historic piazza fountain with water spray, factory chimney steam, and clean driving rewards. |
| **PHASE 10** | Weather Variety & Advanced Special Contracts | ✅ Complete | Dynamic summer rain showers with wet road specular sheen, procedural rain audio synthesis, VIP/Cold-Chain/Heavy-Freight special contracts, and multi-district architectural landmarks (Marina yachts, festive bunting, substation, welcome arch). |
| **PHASE 11** | Night Lighting, Vehicle Headlights & Living Weather | ✅ Complete | Dynamic vehicle headlight beam cones on wet night roads, shop interior window illumination, streetlamp emissive glows, and pedestrian rain reactions (umbrellas). |
| **PHASE 12** | Delivery Dropoff Stations, Customer Visuals & Combo Fanfare | ✅ Complete | 16 physical delivery reception stations (porch, store counter, industrial pallet, marina dock), animated customer NPCs with waving/celebrating behaviors, delivered parcel stacks, radial hold progress ring, and combo streak fanfare. |
| **PHASE 13** | Urban Fauna & Emergent Express Mini-Events | ✅ Complete | Coastal soaring seagulls, piazza feeding pigeons, suburban cats, and dynamic express rush events. |
| **PHASE 14** | Environmental Micro-FX & Fleet Career Milestones | ✅ Complete | District crunchy leaf piles & leaf burst particles, coastal sea spray mist & surf audio, industrial welding workshop with Welder NPC, dynamic arc flicker & sparks, 4 custom vehicle liveries, and company trophies. |
| **PHASE 15** | Urban Traffic Expansion & Pedestrian Signals | ✅ Complete | Animated crosswalk pedestrian traffic signals across 8 intersections, scooter courier AI traffic sharing roads with lean physics & horn salute, and parcel handoff micro-animations with gratitude speech bubbles & squash-and-stretch landing. |
| **PHASE 16** | Audio Jukebox & Transit Infrastructure | ✅ Complete | Procedural in-game car radio channels (Lo-Fi, Synthwave, Funk), animated HUD equalizer pill, 4 district bus stops with "D" transit signs, commuter NPCs, and rooftop solar/satellite/HVAC detailing. |
| **PHASE 17** | Weather Puddle Splashes & Customer Tipping | ✅ Complete | 12 dynamic road puddles across 4 districts, water spray particles, procedural splash audio, hydroplaning micro-feel, customer tipping bonuses (15–30 ₺) with coin chime fanfare & speech bubbles. |
| **PHASE 18** | Fuel Station Pitstops & Street Vending Polish | ✅ Complete | Interactive logistics depot EV quick-charger / fuel pitstop with supercharge buff & unlimited nitro, traditional Turkish street food carts (Çarşı simitçisi & Sahil kestanecisi) with energy buffs & procedural audio. |
| **PHASE 19** | Living Commercial Hubs & Fauna Interactions | ✅ Complete | Full-service highway gas station & convenience store with wash bay, interactive fauna feeding (simit crumbs to pigeons & treats to street dogs/cats). |
| **PHASE 20** | Dynamic Traffic Incident & Road Work Mini-Hazards | ✅ Complete | Dynamic road work hazard zone with asphalt trench, flashing amber warning tower, cones, A-frame barricades, municipal worker NPCs (Kadir Usta & Murat Usta), and safe-speed passing bonus. |
| **PHASE 21** | Seaside Marina Boardwalk Cafe & Fishing NPCs | ✅ Complete | Coastal promenade recreation pier with fishing rod anglers (Salih Reis & Kemal Ağabey), seaside tea garden ("Yalı Çay Bahçesi") with copper samovar & tulip glasses, and moored wooden rowboats ("Meltem" & "Poyraz"). |
| **PHASE 22** | Dynamic Weather Thunderstorm & Lightning Flash System | ✅ Complete | Emergent summer thunderstorms with volumetric heavy rain, 3D lightning bolt, distant procedural rolling thunder audio, dynamic sky flash illumination, and wet puddle expansion. |
| **PHASE 23** | Grand Sahil Ferris Wheel & Amusement Pier Attraction | ✅ Complete | Animated revolving Ferris wheel with illuminated gondolas on the coastal pier, upright gravity counter-rotation, chase lights, ticket booth, operator NPC, and Sahil Neşesi rewards. |

| **PHASE 25** | Industrial Sanayi Freight Rail Spur & Shunting Locomotive | 🔄 Next | Industrial rail spur cutting through Sanayi with crossing gates, flashing red crossbuck signals (`sound.railCrossing()`), diesel shunter locomotive, and heavy flatcars loaded with cargo containers and machinery. |
